Question 1	
Is it relevant to my business?
Yes / No	
Question 2
What does the searcher want?
Informational / Commercial / Transactional / Local
Question 3
How much search demand does it have?

Question 4
Could this searcher become my customer?
High / Medium / Low
That's the core of keyword analysis.

Keyword Analysis (Short Summary)
•	Keyword: What people search on Google. 
•	Keyword Research: Finding and analyzing those searches. 
•	Seed Keyword: Starting keyword, e.g. electrical shop. 
•	Search Intent: Informational, Commercial, Transactional, Local. 
•	Search Volume: Approximate number of searches. 
•	Competition: Mainly Google Ads competition in Keyword Planner. 
•	Relevance: Is the keyword related to our business? 
•	Business Value: Can it bring potential customers? 
•	Primary Keyword: Main keyword/topic of a page. 
•	Secondary Keywords: Related supporting keywords. 
•	Keyword Mapping: Assigning keywords to the correct website pages. 
•	Keyword Placement: Title, H1, content, meta description, etc., naturally. 
•	Main rule: Relevance + Intent + Business Value > just high search volume. 
Process:
Find → Analyze → Filter → Group → Primary/Secondary → Map to Pages

Lesson – 3 : Competitor Analysis , Title & Meta Description Creation
Competitor analysis  : means studying businesses that compete with you to understand what they are doing online.
 

Back link : A backlink is when another website links to your website. It brings referral visitors to your page and also acts as a signal of SEO
SEO Title :  the title of a webpage that tells Google and users what the page is about.
•	50 – 60 characters    ex: Electrical Shop in Hanamkonda | Electrical & Hardware
•	primary keyword should usually appear in the title.
•	Put Important Words Near the Beginning  ex : Electrical shop in Hnk | ABC Electricals  is better than ABC Electricals | Products & services | Electrical shop in hanmakonda
•	Make Every Page's Title Different that should reflect the purpose of each page  Ex:  plumbing , hardware , etc 
•	Don’t stuff the keywords (keyword stuffing)
•	Add business name on important pages like home page 
•	Make it understandable and readable for users
•	Avoid false promises and info about products , costs etc
•	Simple way to make a seo title : Primary Topic/Keyword + Location (when relevant) + Business Name

Meta description : a short description that explains what the webpage offers and encourages the user to click.
•	150 – 160 characters
•	 Keep it relevant to the page 
•	Include the main topic naturally 
•	 Write for humans, not just Google 
•	Don't stuff keywords 
•	Make every page's description different 
•	Give a reason to click
•	Simple trick : What you offer + Location + Benefit/Reason to visit
