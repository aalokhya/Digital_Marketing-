Connect Your Website & Verify Ownership (google search console )

Verify ownership means
Google needs to confirm: “You actually control this website.”
Only after verification can you see private Search Console data and manage settings for the site.
How does Google verify ownership?
Common methods include:	
•	HTML file upload
•	HTML meta tag
•	Google Analytics
•	Google Tag Manager
•	DNS record
For a website where you control the HTML, the HTML meta-tag method can be particularly convenient.
 example, Google may give you something like:
<meta name="google-site-verification" content="XXXXXXXXXXXX">
You place it inside the <head> of your website.
Then Google checks your website and confirms the verification.

Verification:
Google confirms that you control the website.
Indexing:
Google discovers, processes, and potentially includes your pages in its search index.
So:
Verify ownership → Submit sitemap → Google crawls pages → Pages may be indexed → Search performance data appears
