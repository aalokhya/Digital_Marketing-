# 📚 Off-Page SEO Notes — Lesson: Submission Techniques

## 1. Quora Submission (Q&A)

Quora is a question-and-answer platform where users ask questions and share answers on different topics.

### Purpose:

* Increase online visibility

* Reach a relevant audience

* Generate referral traffic

* Build credibility and expertise

* Share relevant website links where permitted



## 2. Forum Submission

Forum submission means participating in online discussion forums by creating threads or replying to existing discussions.

### Purpose:

* Build online presence

* Participate in niche-related discussions

* Generate referral traffic

* Build relationships with communities

* Share useful resources

### Examples covered in class:

SEO Khazana, Warrior Forum, Internet marketing and online business, BlackHatWorld, SEO, internet marketing, and black-hat SEO discussions


## 3. Blog Commenting

Blog commenting means posting meaningful comments on relevant blog articles.

### Purpose:

* Engage with blog authors and readers

* Build relationships

* Increase online visibility

* Generate referral traffic

* Share relevant resources


### Example:

Blog topic: 10 Tips for Better Content Writing

Comment:

"These tips are helpful, especially the point about understanding the target audience before writing. Audience-focused content makes articles more engaging and useful."

SEO Note: Blog comments should contribute to the discussion rather than exist solely to obtain backlinks.

## 4. Video Submission – YouTube & Vimeo

Video submission means uploading and publishing video content on video-sharing platforms.

### Platforms:

* YouTube

* Vimeo

### Purpose:

* Increase content visibility

* Reach video audiences

* Generate referral traffic

* Promote products, services, or brands

* Build an online portfolio

### Important video elements:

* Video title

* Description

* Relevant keywords

* Thumbnail

* Tags or metadata where supported

* Captions/subtitles

* Relevant website links where permitted



# 5. Press Release Submission

Press release submission means publishing or distributing an official announcement about a business, organization, product, service, event, or important achievement through press release platforms.

### Purpose:

* Increase brand awareness

* Share business announcements

* Reach journalists and relevant audiences

* Generate referral traffic

* Build online visibility

* Potentially earn media coverage and relevant mentions

### Examples covered in class:

1. PRLog
2. 1888 Press Release
3. 24-7 Press Release


Remember: Off-page SEO focuses on building online visibility, reputation, relevant mentions, referral traffic, and quality links—not simply submitting content to as many websites as possible.


Quora Submission:     Answering questions on a Q&A platform

Forum Submission:     Participating in online discussions

Blog Commenting:      Posting meaningful comments on blog articles

Video Submission:     Uploading videos to platforms like YouTube and Vimeo

Press Release Submission:  Publishing official announcements through press release platforms
